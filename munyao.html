Moses Ngumbau Munyao

Acting Chairperson, YoungStars Hope Foundation–Africa

About Moses

Moses Ngumbau Munyao is dedicated to unlocking potential and restoring hope by providing spiritual and moral mentorship, leadership development, and life coaching, with added experience in health, research, and youth leadership. He believes in raising visionary, morally grounded youth who will lead meaningful impact in society.

Professional Experience

  • Leadership & Governance: Certified leadership experience as a Christian Union evangelistic team leader, music leader, and chairperson.
  • Youth Mentorship: Trained in mental health awareness through PDO Kenya. Passionate about personal growth, life coaching, and guiding young people.
  • Community Service & Public Health: Experience as a Medical Representative with expertise in health education, and a research background from KEMRI Wellcome Trust in bioscience and laboratory work.
  • Skills Development: Strong skills in planning, teamwork, problem-solving, data analysis, and building partnerships with institutions and communities.
  • Core Values: Faith-rooted leadership, integrity, and commitment to social impact and environmental conservation.
