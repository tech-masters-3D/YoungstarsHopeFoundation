Program Updates June 12, 2026

New STEM Lab Opens Its Doors In Nairobi

Our second STEM Engagement center is now open, doubling the number of students who can access hands-on computing and robotics sessions every Saturday.

After eighteen months of fundraising and planning, our new Nairobi STEM lab officially opened its doors this month. The center adds twelve additional workstations, a small robotics corner, and a dedicated space for our weekend STEM Saturday sessions — effectively doubling the number of students our STEM Engagement program can serve each term.

The lab was made possible through a combination of individual donor contributions, a local business partnership that supplied refurbished computers, and volunteer hours from three former STEM Saturday students who helped set up the space themselves.

Enrollment for the next STEM Saturday intake at the Nairobi location opens shortly — interested families can reach out through our contact page to be added to the waiting list.

Events May 28, 2026

Community Outreach Day Reaches Over 400 Families

Our largest outreach event yet combined health screenings, school enrollment support, and a free lunch for families across three villages.

Volunteers, partner health workers, and program staff spent a full Saturday running a combined outreach event across three villages outside Kitui town. Alongside basic health screenings, the team helped several families complete late school enrollment paperwork for the upcoming term.

Local leaders have already asked us to make the event an annual fixture — planning for next year's outreach day begins later this year.

Volunteer Applications Are Open For Our Next Cohort

We're recruiting mentors, tutors, and event volunteers for the second half of the year — no experience required for most roles.

With two new programs launching this year, we're looking to grow our volunteer base across mentoring, academic tutoring, and event support roles. Orientation sessions for accepted volunteers will run over two weekends before placements begin.

Ready to apply? Visit our Get Involved page to submit your application.

Program Updates April 30, 2026

Talent Development Cohort Holds First Showcase

Twenty-two students from our Talent Development program presented original projects — from short films to small business pitches — to a room of parents and mentors.

The showcase capped off a ten-week term where students chose their own creative or entrepreneurial project to develop with a mentor's guidance. Presentations ranged from a short documentary about local water access to a working prototype for a low-cost phone charging stand.

Several parents in attendance said it was the first time they'd seen their child present in front of an audience — a small but meaningful confidence milestone the program is built around.

Press April 18, 2026

New Local Partnership To Supply STEM Equipment

A Nairobi-based technology retailer has agreed to donate refurbished computers and provide discounted equipment for future program expansion.

The partnership will supply refurbished laptops and desktop computers to our STEM Engagement centers on an ongoing basis, along with discounted pricing on future equipment purchases as our programs grow.

This is our first formal corporate partnership of its kind, and we're actively exploring similar arrangements with other local businesses — reach out if your organization is interested in partnering with us.

Announcements April 2, 2026

18 Students Complete Academic Training Program

Our largest graduating cohort yet received certificates at a small ceremony attended by families, mentors, and program staff.

Eighteen students completed the full year of academic coaching, study skills training, and mentorship under our Academic Training program — the largest single cohort since the program began.

Several graduates have already secured places in technical colleges and universities for the coming year. We'll be following up with a few of their stories in future posts.

Program Updates March 20, 2026

Mentorship Circles Expand To Two New Schools

Fifteen newly trained mentors are now paired with students at two additional partner schools, extending our reach beyond Kitui town.

Following a three-week mentor training course, fifteen new volunteer mentors have been paired with students at two partner schools that joined our network this term. Each mentor commits to weekly check-ins for the full school year.

Interested in becoming a mentor yourself? Details are on our Get Involved page.

Press March 5, 2026

Foundation Receives First Institutional Grant

A one-year grant will fund equipment and mentor stipends across our STEM Engagement and Talent Development programs.

This marks our first grant from an institutional funder, and will directly support equipment costs and mentor stipends across two of our four core programs over the coming year.

We're grateful for the confidence this represents, and plan to share a full transparency report on how these funds are used once the grant period begins.
